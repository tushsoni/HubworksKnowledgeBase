Zip Inventory: everything in one place

How Zip Inventory works end to end: the setup model, counts, deliveries, transfers, waste, sales and recipes, and how they become Actual Usage, Ideal (Theoretical) Usage and Variance. Every formula has a calculator, and every topic has ready-to-copy PostgreSQL queries driven by the customer email and store ID.

How the numbers flow

Core formulas at a glance

Actual Usage

Actual = Opening + Transfer In + Deliveries − Closing − Transfer Out
Qty rolls up per ingredient; $ is valued per supplier item (SKU) cost.

Ideal / Theoretical

Theo = Σ (menu items sold × recipe qty) + theoretical waste
From posted sales mix and recipes.

Variance

ACTMINTHEO (default): Variance = Actual − Theo THEOMINACT: Variance = Theo − Actual Variance % = Variance $ ÷ Net sales × 100

Count line value

Line $ = count (base) × case cost ÷ base units per case case cost = eositevit.lastdvlunitcost (last delivery price)
A line with no cost is valued at $0, which is the most common cause of a wrong Actual $.
